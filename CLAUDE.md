Closed-loop speed control firmware for an STM32G474 driving a PMDC motor (M4-4205D) with tachogenerator feedback. The analog frontend is already built: bipolar tach signal conditioned to a VDDA/2-centered 0.21–3.09 V range on the ADC, ~1.8 rpm per 12-bit code, so the ADC reading is signed speed with direction included. There's an existing motor driver in this workspace — read its header/API first. Goal: a periodic control task at a 1 kHz tick that samples the tach, converts to signed rpm, computes error against a setpoint, runs a PI controller with anti-windup, saturates the output, and calls the driver to hold constant speed. Targeting 50–100 Hz loop bandwidth.
